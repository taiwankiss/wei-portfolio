貝殼似顏繪

BrandingGraphic

專案介紹

本專案為任職於「貝殼放大」期間，為全體團隊成員量身打造的 Q 版人像插畫與生日祝福卡片系統（Team Character Design）。

專案以活潑亮眼的品牌紫、黃為主色調，結合像素風格（Pixel Style）字體與扁平化插畫語彙，精準抓取數十位夥伴的髮型、眼鏡、個人配件與神韻特質（甚至包含辦公室寵物）。這套頭像不僅應用於團隊生日祝福小卡，更成為凝聚內部企業文化、展現團隊活力的視覺資產。

Project Overview

A passion-driven character design project created during my time at Backer-Founder, where I illustrated customized vector avatars and birthday cards for all team members.

Designed using a vibrant brand-color palette (purple & yellow) and retro pixel typography, each avatar encapsulates unique personal features, hairstyles, accessories, and distinct expressions—even incorporating office pets. This system was used for internal birthday greeting cards and culture-building, showcasing a warm, collaborative, and creative workplace environment.
