拒絕血汗海鮮懶人包

專案介紹

本專案為關注遠洋漁業「人權剝削」與「海洋生態破壞」所製作的社會倡議資訊設計。

專案針對台灣遠洋漁船上的外籍漁工權益、海上奴役現象以及美國「黃牌 warnings」等國際外交與經濟影響進行系統性梳理。設計上採用具故事繪本質感的插畫風格，以深海藍為主調，搭配警示感強烈烈的紅橙色系，將龐雜的國際法規與產業痛點，拆解為四大層面（生態破壞、海上奴役、守法業者衝擊、台灣國際形象），以層次分明、引人深思的視覺語言引發社會關注與公眾倡議。

Overview

A social advocacy campaign designed to raise awareness about forced labor and ecological destruction in the distant-water fishing industry.

The project breaks down complex international human rights issues—such as the exploitation of migrant fishers and global trade warnings—into an engaging, story-driven information architecture. Utilizing a rich, narrative illustration style with deep oceanic blues and striking warning tones, the design highlights key issues across four main pillars, transforming heavyweight policy and ethical topics into a visually impactful social campaign.
