專案介紹

本專案為上市公司「九暘電子（IC Plus）」所打造的 B2B 企業官方網站 UI/UX 設計與資訊架構企劃。

因應半導體與 IC 設計領域的專業採購與工程師需求，本專案打破傳統科技公司生硬龐雜的資訊層級，以「極簡、高效、精準檢索」為核心策略。設計上梳理了龐大的產品型號與規格數據，打造直觀的產品對照表與應用領域分類介面；視覺上採用穩重的科技深藍與清晰的層次排版，兼顧上市公司應有的專業信賴感與極佳的使用者體驗（User Experience）。

Overview

A B2B corporate website UI/UX redesign created for IC Plus Corporation, a publicly traded semiconductor and IC design company.

Designed to streamline the technical user journey for engineers and buyers, the visual strategy focuses on clarity, efficiency, and structured information architecture. The interface reorganizes extensive product lines and technical datasheets into clean, easily scannable data tables and application-based navigation. Featuring a refined tech-blue palette and precise grid layouts, the website delivers a seamless user experience that reinforces the enterprise’s technological authority and reliability.
