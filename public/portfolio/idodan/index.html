專案介紹

本專案為台灣原創木質腕錶品牌「Idodan」所規劃的 戶外商業產品攝影與風格人像視覺企劃。

拍攝策略以「回歸自然與時間對話」為主軸，特別選址於自然森林與樹林場景。透過將木質腕錶置於青苔、年輪木樁與天然樹皮上，完美勾勒出原木紋理與手作工藝的溫潤質感；同步企劃文青風格的大地色系模特兒配戴與靜態書寫情境，為品牌打造極具沉浸感、慢活氣息與自然美學的全方位行銷視覺庫。

Overview

An outdoor commercial photography and lifestyle direction project created for Idodan, a brand known for its handcrafted wooden watches.

Inspired by the connection between craftsmanship and nature, the visual direction locates the product within lush forest environments. Placing the timepieces against mossy tree trunks, weathered stumps, and organic bark highlights the unique wooden textures and artisan details. Paired with soft natural lighting and earth-toned lifestyle portraits, the photo suite creates a serene, artistic story that strongly elevates the brand’s organic identity.
