USPACE JP 名片

Branding

專案介紹

本專案為共享智慧出行品牌「USPACE」日本分部所使用的名片系統設計。

設計針對日本商務市場注重「嚴謹秩序」與「專業信賴」的文化特性，建立「極致黑（Black）」與「純粹白（White）」雙色名片體系。排版上融合日英文資訊的網格架構（Grid System），精準呈現株式會社名稱、職銜與聯絡細節，並透過極簡的幾何線條與現代留白，展現 USPACE 作為跨國科技品牌高雅、前瞻且值得信賴的商業形象。

Project Overview

A corporate business card and identity extension designed for USPACE as part of its brand expansion into the Japanese market.

Tailored specifically to the refined corporate etiquette of Japan, the design features a dual colorway—Sleek Black and Minimalist White. Utilizing a structured grid system, the layout effortlessly balances bilingual information (Japanese & English) with ample white space. The result is a clean, modern, and highly professional touchpoint that reinforces USPACE’s credibility as an innovative global mobility platform.
