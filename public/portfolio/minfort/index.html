Minfort Min7 Bluetooth Photo

Photography

專案介紹

本專案為台灣手工音響品牌 Minfort 旗下的旗艦產品「MIN7 實木藍牙音響」所規劃的 商業產品攝影與情境藝術指導。

攝影策略以「工藝美學融入日常生活」為主軸，透過暖調光影精準勾勒手工實木的溫潤紋理與金屬單體細節。同步企劃「現代創作者工作桌」與「自然綠意家居」雙重情境，將精品音響轉化為提升空間質感的家居藝術品，為集資推廣與品牌官網建立強烈且具溫度的視覺印象。

Project Overview

A commercial photography and lifestyle direction project for Minfort MIN7, a premium handcrafted wooden Bluetooth speaker.

The visual direction centers on merging artisanal craft with modern lifestyle interior design. By capturing the natural wood grain textures under warm ambient light, the photography highlights the speaker’s acoustic craftsmanship. Curated scenarios—ranging from sleek iMac workspaces to vibrant, plant-filled living areas—position the MIN7 not just as an audio device, but as a statement piece for sophisticated home decor.
