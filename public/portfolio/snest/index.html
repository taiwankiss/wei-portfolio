專案介紹

本專案為生醫科技設備 S.NEST（高通量細胞增生培養系統） 所規劃的 商業產品攝影與實驗室情境藝術指導。

針對高階醫療與生物科技設備極致追求「精準、無菌、權威感」的視覺需求，本專案採用高光調（High-Key）光影控制，塑造臨床級無塵室的極簡潔淨感。透過高精度微距畫面，細膩刻劃設備的極簡金屬質感與手部操作細節（包含分液滴定、微孔板載入與數據分析情境），精準轉化複雜的生醫技術為具備國際級說服力的視覺資產。

Overview

A commercial biotech photography and art direction project created for S.NEST, a high-throughput cell culture and expansion system.

Designed to align with top-tier biomedical and pharmaceutical standards, the visual strategy emphasizes sterility, precision, and clinical authority. Utilizing high-key lighting to construct a seamless cleanroom environment, the photography captures precise human-device interactions—such as pipetting, microplate loading, and data analysis. The images elevate a complex scientific instrument into an approachable, high-tech, and globally trusted medical solution.
