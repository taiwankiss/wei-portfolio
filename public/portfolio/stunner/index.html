Bistro Stunner Photo

Photography

專案介紹

本專案為獨立餐酒館（Bistro）量身打造全套視覺攝影企劃，範疇涵蓋品牌招牌料理與特色酒水。影像以不同視角多元呈現，結合沉穩溫暖的木質基調與層次豐富的自然側光，襯托出食材的鮮美質感與料理的澎湃豐盛感。整體風格兼具高質感與歡聚氛圍，專為菜單設計、品牌官網及社群行銷所規劃。

Project Overview

A commercial food & beverage photography series crafted for a local bistro. Utilizing top-down flatlay compositions, rich color tones, and warm ambient lighting, the visuals highlight the textures, vibrant colors, and convivial atmosphere of the culinary offerings. Designed specifically for menu design, website showcases, and social media campaigns.
