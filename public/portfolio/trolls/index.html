專案介紹

本專案為結合手機 App 互動的智慧絨毛玩具（Smart Plush Toy）進行全套商業攝影企劃。視覺策略著重於 「實體玩偶與數位介面（App UI）的自然互動」。企劃涵蓋室內棚拍（Studio Lifestyle）與戶外攜帶情境（Outdoor On-the-Go），精準呈現玩具在居家陪護、AR 互動辨識以及戶外攜帶等多元使用場景，完美融合產品的可愛質感與科技互動感。

Overview

A commercial photography campaign for an App-connected interactive plush toy. The project highlights the seamless integration between the physical product and digital mobile experience. Through studio lifestyle setups and outdoor photography, the visuals capture real-time interaction, mobile AR scanning, and portable playfulness, presenting the toy as an engaging, everyday companion for modern users.
