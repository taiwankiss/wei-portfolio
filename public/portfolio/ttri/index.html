高間距織品懶人包

Graphic

專案介紹

本專案為紡織產業綜合研究所（TTRI）所進行的 產業技術資訊圖表與品牌倡議視覺設計。

專案針對艱深的 B2B 技術——「高間距織物（Spacer Fabric）」技術，以極簡現代風格的手繪插畫進行重新梳理。設計上拆解了複雜的微觀織物結構剖面圖，並具象化其在「單人立槳（SUP）、充氣浮橋、高噸位千斤頂」等多種極限情境下的創新應用。同步為其 R&D 100 大獎與「口罩國家隊」國家級倡議製作主題視覺，將冰冷的產業研發成果轉化為兼具專業質感、科普親和力與社會影響力的視覺資產。

Project Overview

A comprehensive industrial infographic and technical branding project for the Taiwan Textile Research Institute (TTRI).

The project focuses on illustrating and simplifying complex B2B textile technologies, specifically advanced spacer fabric structures. By translating technical specifications—such as R&D 100 award-winning DIFA structure analysis and varied applications in SUP boards, pneumatic jacks, and bridges—into intuitive vector diagrams and lifestyle illustrations, the design transforms a specialized subject into an approachable and informative visual narrative. The project also included strategic branding and patriotic illustrations for the national ‘mask team’ campaign, strengthening TTRI’s social impact and visibility.
